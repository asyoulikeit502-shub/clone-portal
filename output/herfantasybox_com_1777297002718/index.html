Can Chlorophyll Help Me Feel Fresher?

How does chlorophyll deodorize the body?

The theory is that chlorophyll deodorizes the body by detoxifying the digestive tract, neutralizing odors, and supporting a healthy microbiome. Chlorophyll is also known to have antioxidant properties that can help eliminate toxins that contribute to body odor.

Why does sweat sometimes smell like onions?

Everyone’s body chemistry is unique, and certain foods — like garlic and onions — can sometimes influence how sweat smells. When these foods are digested, natural compounds may interact with the skin’s bacteria, creating a scent that’s stronger or different than usual. Factors like diet, lifestyle, and individual body chemistry can all play a role.

If you are pregnant or nursing, it’s important to consult your healthcare provider before using any dietary supplement, including chlorophyll. Only your doctor can provide guidance based on your individual health needs.
